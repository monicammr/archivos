All formal proofs are collected in the folder \texttt{lean\_proofs/} of the reproducibility package, a self-contained Lean~4 project (Lean 4.23.0, Mathlib v4.23.0) that compiles with a single command (\texttt{./verify.sh}). It contains 32 theory modules with 309 theorems and lemmas, and 28 benchmark models translated from SBML. No file contains \texttt{sorry} or \texttt{axiom}, and \texttt{\#print axioms} reports only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} for every theorem. Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:modules} lists the modules in the logical order of the proofs: \# is the step number, and every module only uses modules with a smaller number (column `Uses'). $n$ is the number of theorems and lemmas in the module (its size, not an index). \sref{stmts} gives the statement and meaning of every theorem and lemma; the file \texttt{THEOREMS.md} in the folder lists them as well.

\textbf{Verified benchmark models (28).} Armistead, Bachmann, Bertozzi, Blasi, Boehm, Borghans, Brannmark, Bruno, Chen (501 states, in four parts), Crauste (horizon $T\le 1$), Elowitz, Fiedler, Froehlich (1,228 states, in twelve parts), Fujita, Giordano, Isensee, Lang, Laske, Lucarelli, Okuonghae, Rahman, Raia, Raimundez, SalazarCavazos, Sneyd, Weber, Zhao and Zheng (folder \texttt{lean\_proofs/Models/}). For each model, the final theorem establishes that the vector field is smooth on its domain, that the solution exists on the simulated horizon and that concentrations remain non-negative. The data of each model and the checks executed are given in \sref{models} and Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:models}, which also explain why the remaining seven benchmark problems were not verified.
