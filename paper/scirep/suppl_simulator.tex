\snote{sim}

Our simulation of the measured outputs (\texttt{certificados/petab\_outputs.py}, libRoadRunner with CVODE, relative and absolute tolerances $10^{-7}$ and $10^{-10}$) was compared, at the nominal parameters $\theta_0$, with the reference simulations distributed with the benchmark (file \texttt{simulations.tsv} of each problem), matching every measurement by observable, experimental condition and time. For measurement $i$ the relative difference is $|\hat y_i-y^{\mathrm{ref}}_i|/\max(|y^{\mathrm{ref}}_i|,10^{-12})$; Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:simulator} gives its median and maximum for every problem.

In all problems with a comparable reference the median difference is below $10^{-5}$ except Chen ($1.1\times10^{-4}$), Crauste (0.8\%, a very stiff model), Lang, Perelson and Zheng. In Lang, an oscillating cell-cycle model simulated to $t=136{,}472$, the median difference is 2.8\% and the peaks differ by an accumulated phase shift. In Perelson the difference is 17\%; the initial state of the SBML model is stationary and our simulation respects it, so the discrepancy most likely lies in the reference. In Zheng the reference file contains the measured data rather than a simulation. Lang and Perelson were kept in the analysis, and their results should be read with this caveat. Problems without a comparable reference have no file in the benchmark or use different condition identifiers.
