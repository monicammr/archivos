Fig. 3 | Prediction of held-out experiments with real data. For each problem, the ratio of the test chi2/n (mean squared noise-scaled residual on the held-out experiments) of the reduced model to that of a reference, on a logarithmic scale. a, Reference: the full model re-estimated on the same training data (n = 25 problems). b, Reference: the published estimate theta0, which was obtained using all data including the test experiments (n = 27). Ratios below 1 (left of the vertical line, blue-green circles) mean that the reduced model predicts better; the grey band marks equivalence within 5% (dark squares) and orange triangles mark worse predictions. Labels give the ratio; brown diamonds flag problems whose reduced test chi2/n exceeds 10, where a favourable ratio does not imply a good absolute fit. chi2/n of about 1 indicates residuals comparable to the reported measurement noise. Problems without a training/test split (Bertozzi, Blasi), without a full re-estimation (Chen, Lang; panel a only) and with noise-free data (test chi2/n of theta0 below 0.01: Giordano, Perelson, Rahman) are not shown.
