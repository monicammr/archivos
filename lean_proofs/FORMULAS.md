### Step 31. `IntervalInit` (5)

Parameter-dependent initial values certified with rational interval arithmetic.

Notation: $[l,u]$ (`ival θq e = some (l, u)`): rational interval enclosing the value of e at θ_q; $\mathrm{checkInitI}$ (`checkInitI sx θq G`): interval check that G(θ_q) ≥ 0 and > 0 on Σ.
